Experiment No. 10 Storytelling in Tableau

Aim To create a story in Tableau by combining multiple visualizations into a sequence of insights.

Description: Data storytelling is the process of presenting data through a sequence of visualizations and explanations. Tableau Stories allow users to arrange different visualizations as story points so that viewers can understand the analysis step by step.

Uses: • Presenting analytical findings clearly. • Explaining trends and patterns step by step. • Combining charts into a logical sequence. • Supporting business presentations. • Communicating insights to non-technical users.

Algorithm:

Open Tableau Public.
Connect to the dataset.
Create multiple worksheets.
Create a dashboard if required.
Open the Story tab.
Add story points.
Arrange visualizations sequentially.
Add captions for explanation.
Analyze and save the story.
